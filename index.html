03 Texture to paint Experimental

Make a static, limited-color snapshot. Original models remain untouched. Opaque textures and authored colors only.

High contrast is preserved, including high-contrast noise. Detail is bounded; small features below the selected raster resolution may disappear. No mesh repair or printability guarantee.

Method and references

Independent area-weighted perceptual quantization, contour clipping, and graph-based cleanup. See the algorithm and benchmark. The attributed Texture2Paint slicer writer is reused; its broader decal, repair and printer workflows are not duplicated.
